BIPV Heat Research Project

Building Integrated Photovoltaic Heat Analysis

Advancing sustainable building technologies through comprehensive thermal analysis

Project Overview

This research project focuses on understanding the thermal behavior of facade Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) systems. Our comprehensive analysis includes:

  • • Outdoor Thermal Comfort: Assessing radiant energy and thermal comfort in the nearby proximity to a BIPV facade
  • • Thermal Performance Analysis: Comparing a BIPV facade and common facade systems
  • • BIPV Ventilation Design: Measuring heat characteristics of a ventilated BIPV facade within an air cavity
  • • Data-Driven Insights: Developing an advanced sensor network with real-time monitoring
  • • Experimental Solutions: Characterising the state-of-the-art facility relative to BIPV development
