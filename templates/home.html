Project Overview

This research project focuses on understanding the thermal behavior of Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) systems. Our comprehensive analysis includes:

  • • Thermal Performance Analysis: Measuring heat transfer characteristics of BIPV installations
  • • Environmental Impact: Assessing energy efficiency and thermal comfort
  • • Data-Driven Insights: Advanced sensor networks and real-time monitoring
  • • Sustainable Solutions: Optimizing building energy performance
